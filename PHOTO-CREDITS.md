# Illustrative Ithaca mosaic — photo credits

These six photographs are illustrative demo images from Wikimedia Commons. They are not live community contributions, and their photographers have not endorsed Paint Ithaca. Suggested visible caption: **Illustrative Ithaca photos · Photo credits**.

The files in `assets/mosaic/` are Wikimedia's JPEG thumbnails, downloaded on September 14, 2026. They retain their original compositions and colors; only the Wikimedia thumbnail resizing has been applied to the stored files. The interface may crop or apply its watercolor treatment to these demo images. Source photographs and any such visual adaptations retain the licenses listed below.
